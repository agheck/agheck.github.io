Run, Hide, Activate

The Run, Hide, Activate system addresses the critical issue of active shooter incidents through an innovative response system. This project has reached many milestones. Most notably, there are two fully functional distraction devices, a fully functional central server, and a fully functional activation mechanism.

The distraction devices each have three distractions: flashing lights, playing loud noises, and shooting marbles. They send real-time information back to the central server, allowing administrators and law enforcement to track the shooter through the building.

The central server is the communication hub and keeps track of system information. It stores system status, log files from the distraction devices, and a map showing where the distraction devices are and whether or not they detect a shooter.



Communication between all devices is seamless. All devices communicate over MQTT with a QoS of one, meaning that the message is guaranteed to be received. Additionally, since MQTT is designed for areas with bad networks, it should work in nearly every situation.
